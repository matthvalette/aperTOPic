Topic probabilities, explained

Give an affair, a document or any text. Jev answers one yes/no question per topic and returns a probability for each one. Topics above the threshold are assigned.

The 28 Curia Vista subject areas are the default: they are the labelled set, so their accuracy is known (top topic correct for about 92% of affairs).
